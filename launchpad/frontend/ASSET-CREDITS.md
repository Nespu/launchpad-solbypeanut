# 3D asset credits

The welcome-screen coin background uses these downloaded GLB assets:

- `public/models/coin.glb` — “Gold-coin, Coin, Money, Currency, Blank” by Plaggy_CC0 on Pixabay: https://pixabay.com/3d-models/gold-coin-coin-money-currency-528/
- `public/models/shiba.glb` — “Shiba-inu, Dog, Animal, Brown, White” by Quaternius_CC0 on Pixabay: https://pixabay.com/3d-models/shiba-inu-dog-animal-brown-white-1332/

Both listings identify the assets as free under the Pixabay Content License. Attribution is not required; this file records their sources. The coin GLB was adapted for web delivery by resizing its embedded 4096px texture maps to 1024px and applying green metallic materials in the renderer. The Shiba model is used as a small relief on the coin face. The background is decorative and only loads on the first-visit introduction.
